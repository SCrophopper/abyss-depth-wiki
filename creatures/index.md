# Creatures

## Cycle Spores

Rick mentions **Cycle Spores** while speaking to Mrs. Selani:

> “Just eat a Cycle Spore, then you'll be freed forever.”

Their true nature remains unknown in the source journal. The quote appears in [Abyss Depth #7: Scorched Earth](https://www.deviantart.com/scrophopper/journal/Abyss-Depth-7-Scorched-Earth-1191672112).

## STAR

STAR is a biological entity or organism documented in [STAR Biological Blueprints]({{ '/creatures/star/' | relative_url }}). The source journal does not yet provide a written description.
