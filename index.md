# Descend into the world of Abyss Depth.

{: .lead }
**Abyss Depth** is a fictional universe created by **SCrophopper**, set after an unprecedented cataclysm transformed Earth into a flooded world. Humanity survives in deep-sea habitats and surface refugee communities while resource scarcity, social conflict, strange creatures, and D.E.E.P.'s hidden activity shape the future.

> **Spoiler warning:** This wiki contains plot, lore, and character information that may affect a first-time experience. Information may change as the story develops.
