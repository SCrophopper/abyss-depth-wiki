# D.E.E.P.

**D.E.E.P.** takes its name from **Depth Exploration and Environmental Program**. It is a giant corporation focused on the exploration and sustainability of deep-sea environments. Its work spans research, technology, and innovation, making it a foremost authority in the underwater world.

## Influence

D.E.E.P. is the public face of underwater advancement. Its mark is visible throughout underwater life—from submersibles exploring deep trenches to the habitats where people live and work. Its research has shaped humanity's understanding of ocean ecosystems.

## Classified questions

An internal D.E.E.P. dossier appears to have been compromised. Rick connects the people he calls the **White Freaks** to the corporation. Their clothing is unlike that of ordinary habitat residents, and they appear to be searching for something important. Myke seems to know more—and has mentioned the narrator directly.

Known D.E.E.P.-associated operators in the source journal include:

- **Blanche** — Alpha class (`α`)
- **Hazel** — Beta class (`β`)
