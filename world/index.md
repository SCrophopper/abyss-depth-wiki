# World

The world of **Abyss Depth** is a flooded Earth where the sea rose to the height of the planet's highest former land point. Atmospheric layers expanded, keeping pressure at the surface survivable. Humanity now lives across surface raft communities, submerged habitats, villages, resorts, dwellings, and a small number of underwater cities.
