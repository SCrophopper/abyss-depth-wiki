# Refugees

**Refugees** are people who build habitations on the new ocean surface. Their communities pursue simpler and more self-sufficient lives, often emphasizing sustainability and efficient use of resources.

[View the early Refugee Camp artwork on DeviantArt](https://www.deviantart.com/scrophopper/art/Refugee-Camp-Early-version-Abyss-Depth-1208510638).

## Community systems

1. **Floating rafts:** Homes are built on large rafts made from durable materials. Rafts can be linked to form larger structures.
2. **Hydroponics:** Controlled farming systems grow vegetables, fruit, and other crops within limited space.
3. **Fish farming:** Enclosed aquaculture systems provide a stable source of protein.
4. **Recycling facilities:** Plastics, metals, and other materials are reprocessed for reuse.
5. **Water collection and purification:** Rain and other sources supply water, which is filtered and purified for consumption.
6. **Livestock:** Some communities raise animals in confined spaces for meat and other products.
7. **Solar and wind energy:** Renewable systems provide power for lighting, heating, and other needs.
8. **Community collaboration:** Groups share responsibility for agriculture, fishing, waste management, and other essential work.
9. **Security measures:** Watchtowers, alarms, and defensive systems protect communities from threats rising from the depths.

These practices allow surface communities to adapt to their unique environment while pursuing a peaceful, sustainable existence.
