# Underwater Habitations

Underwater habitations are self-sustaining submerged structures equipped with technology that maintains livable conditions, supports human life, and enables work and research. They use reinforced materials, environmental controls, hydroponic gardens, aquaculture, and connected transport systems.

- **Underwater cities:** Large metropolises that house major populations. They contain tall structures, transportation, communication, commerce, culture, and waste-management infrastructure. Only a few exist around the world.
- **Underwater habitats:** Interconnected modules or pods with residential, recreational, and agricultural functions. They are widely affordable and can support long-term habitation or tourism.
- **Underwater villages:** Close-knit clusters of homes, shops, and communal facilities linked by walkways or tunnels. Residents depend on shared resources and cooperation.
- **Underwater resorts:** High-end hotels, restaurants, bars, spas, and entertainment venues for wealthy families. Activities include diving, snorkeling, underwater photography, and encounters with marine wildlife.
- **Underwater dwellings:** Individual or family residences ranging from compact structures to large custom homes. They may include transparent walls, integrated hydroponics, and environmental-control technology.
