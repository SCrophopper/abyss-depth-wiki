# In-depth World Description

After the catastrophe, Earth's sea level rose to the same height as the highest point on the former surface. Someone standing on a raft surrounded by ocean would therefore be **8,849 metres above the former land**, yet conditions at the new sea surface feel normal.

Scientists discovered that the troposphere and other atmospheric layers had expanded, restoring normal air pressure at that height. They continued investigating the event's cause while developing the technology required for human survival.

Humanity created facilities, structures, and equipment capable of withstanding immense water pressure. The basic principle nevertheless remains unchanged: **the deeper a person dives, the greater the pressure.**
